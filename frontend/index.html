03 / CARE KNOWLEDGE

Understand the information.

Public listings help you find a starting point, but they do not replace confirming details with a practice.

Provider verification

An NPI identifies a healthcare provider; it does not by itself prove an active license or qualifications. Verify credentials through the appropriate licensing authority.

Insurance coverage

Ask both the practice and your insurer whether the specific clinician and appointment type are in network.

Languages and access

Confirm the clinician's spoken language and ask the organization for qualified interpreting services when needed.

Reviews and appointments

Avoid sharing sensitive health information in public reviews. A practice website does not guarantee scheduling availability.
